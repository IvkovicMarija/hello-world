## 1. Core thesis

> "Ad sets that include a range of formats (video and image), and concepts (different visuals and messaging) give our AI-enabled recommendations engine the ability to deliver industry-leading personalization at scale. **We call this creative diversification.**" — p.4

- Title of p.4: "Diverse ad sets enable the Meta ads ecosystem to match the right ad to the right person at the right time."
- p.8: "Aim for a mix of formats, visuals and messages at the ad set level." Creative diversification "also combats the creative fatigue that can occur when your audience keeps seeing the same creative assets over and over again."
- p.12: "Diversify your formats, visuals and messages at the ad set level" + "Enable personalization of your creatives at the impression level."
- p.19: "**Stop thinking in singles. Start building in systems.**" The move is FROM "one-off assets" TO "a creative operating system".

## 3. Frameworks

### Creative operating system (p.19–20)
**Concepting → Remixing and iteration → Format adaption → Automated personalization** (p.20)
1. **Concepting**: "Develop motivators and build distinct creative against each one, with or without creators."
2. **Remixing and iteration**: "Remix your video to create different hooks, or add new hooks and messages."
3. **Format adaption**: "For each, adapt for 4:5 video and image aspect ratios."
4. **Automated personalization**: "Opt-into a range of generative AI features to amplify the personalisation of each asset." (Advantage+ creative, p.44–48)

On p.20, each concept is a motivator, a barrier or a benefit, and each concept gets Ad + hook 1/2/3.

### Motivators vs demographics (p.21–28)
- "Motivators are insights into what drives your customer. They are the 'why' behind the scroll, the click and the conversion." (p.21)
- Demographics are "External, broad, 'who I am'". Motivators are "Intrinsic, specific, 'how I behave'" (p.22). Example: "27 yo woman interested in beauty" vs "I need evidence as well as peer reviewed experiences to persuade me to try a new product."
- Worksheet columns: MOTIVATOR/BARRIER → PRODUCT OR SERVICE BENEFIT → MESSAGE THEME → TEXT HOOK → CONCEPT (p.25)
- Where to find motivators: start with your product, dive into the comments, institutional knowledge, ask the community, get a bit emotional, competitive white space (p.27)
- Creative white space map (p.24): emotional vs functional, hi-fi vs lo-fi, product vs people, creator vs brand

### Hooks (p.15, p.33–34)
- "Use the first 2 seconds to capture attention through audio, video or text." (p.15)
- "A hook is the first couple of seconds of a video, when we decide whether to keep watching, or whether to keep scrolling." (p.33)
- Good hooks sound like **"A text from a friend" · "A quiet confession" · "A truth they didn't want to say out loud but finally are" · "Like it came from the comment section"** (p.33)
- "Film multiple hooks for each video based on distinct motivators" (p.34)
- Text overlays should use "the language of the platform, not the language of the boardroom" (p.16)

### The 3-format minimum (p.8, p.10, p.36)
- "Always create at least three different formats with a mix of video and image assets": **9:16 video with audio · 4:5 or 1:1 video · 1:1 or 4:5 image** (p.8)
- Shoot 9:16, adapt to 4:5 video, then pull key frames for the image ad. "You can also use those stills for your ad's thumbnail in Ads Manager." (p.36)
- Turn on Advantage+ placements, or manually select at least 6 placements (p.7, p.8)

### Reels creative essentials (p.14–15)
- Built for **vertical** · Built with **audio** · Built in the **safe zone** (p.14)
- Extra elements: Hooks · Visual dynamism ("multiple fast shots… or transitions such as match cuts") · Audio techniques · Text overlays ("keep your messages inside the safe zone") · Human presence ("real people such as customers, creators or employees") (p.15)
- Image ads, the "digital billboard" (p.16): bold, centered subject; brief; rewrite the headline as a hook; human presence; branding "clear, but integrated", using "brand elements beyond your logo such as colour, typography or shapes."

### Note on safe-zone numbers
The PDF says to build inside the safe zone, but it **does not give pixel or percentage values**. The "top ~14% / bottom ~35% / right edge" values in `video_prompt.md` come from the brief, not from the PDF.
